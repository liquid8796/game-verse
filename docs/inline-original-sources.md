# Contextual figures for the original stories

The additions in `src/features/articles/lib/inline-original.ts` sit within the body at the exact named section and paragraph. They supplement the existing four explanatory figures for VALORANT, Counter-Strike 2, Fortnite and release planning. None repeats an article cover.

The five publisher images were downloaded from the primary pages below, visually inspected, then saved as WebP with Sharp at quality 84 and a maximum width of 1600px. Full proportions are preserved; no cropping, compositing or generated gameplay is used. Source files were staged outside the repository. The two Rockstar images are officially released promotional screenshots; the Mojang and Genshin scenes are archival and described as such in captions.

## League farming figure

`league-last-hit.svg` is an original, code-native GameVerse illustration, 1200 × 720. It appears after paragraph 1 of “Practice getting paid”. The two conceptual minions explain waiting until an attack can remove the remaining health; the bars do not prescribe numerical thresholds, current champion damage or item stats. Its caption distinguishes farming lane responsibilities from support income. The explanatory source is [Riot's beginner guide](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/how-to-play/); there is no original remote asset for this authored SVG.

The drawing was rasterized to a temporary PNG for visual inspection. Article metadata includes intrinsic dimensions, descriptive alt text, a contextual caption and visible credit for every figure.
