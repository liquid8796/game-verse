# RPG story depth and contextual figures

Reviewed on 10 October 2026. This patch deepens the eight existing RPG guides. It preserves game records, article identifiers, slugs, authors, publication dates, existing headings, existing source links, and all existing body figures. Reading times are calculated by the existing helper from the expanded prose.

All eight new figures are original native SVG explanations. They contain no copied interface, generated gameplay, or invented game screenshot. Diagram routes, positions, attack sequences, and the Hades II/Baldur's Gate 3 numerical examples are explicitly hypothetical. Existing official game photographs remain alongside them. SVGs keep their intrinsic aspect ratios and total approximately 20 KB; there are no new raster downloads.

## Asset and placement verification

Each SVG includes a title, description, fixed intrinsic dimensions and a viewBox. All eight were rasterized with Sharp and visually inspected together. The Elden Ring junction label and Cyberpunk entrance label were moved after inspection to keep them off the route strokes. Text labels remain inside their canvas and the source SVGs preserve vector sharpness.

New assets are in `public/article-media/body/deeper-rpg/`; registry entries are in `src/features/articles/lib/inline-rpg.ts`. Paragraph indexes are one-based and refer to the exact paragraphs listed above. Old figure positions remain valid because the original section paragraphs were retained. All eight articles now have two or three body figures in total.

No performance benchmark, gameplay recording, or installed-game playthrough is claimed. The diagrams are reading aids for the worked situations, not empirical measurements of game balance.
