# Catalog expansion image sources

Verified and downloaded on October 7, 2026. All sixteen cover images come from the developer or publisher's official Steam store gallery. The original URLs were obtained from each app's public Steam store API, using the full-size screenshot field rather than the thumbnail field.

The files are saved under `public/game-media/expansion`. Game cards and hubs reuse the same cover as their corresponding story; `src/features/games/lib/expansion-artwork.ts` holds the source link, credit, descriptive alt text, caption and measured output dimensions.

These are official in-game gallery stills and screenshots, including cinematic camera angles chosen by the publisher. They are not original GameVerse captures or generated gameplay. The source galleries do not identify capture dates or build versions, so the captions do not present their interfaces or visuals as proof of a particular current patch. No promotional artwork is labeled as a gameplay capture.

Full-size originals and saved WebP files were visually inspected. The saved files preserve the originals' proportions, have a maximum width of 1600 pixels, and use Sharp WebP quality 85. Sea of Thieves is 1600 x 901 because the original is 1918 x 1080; the other fifteen are 1600 x 900 from 1920 x 1080 originals. Combined saved size: 3.70 MiB.
