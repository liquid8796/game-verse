# GameVerse catalog expansion — October 7, 2026

This release adds sixteen games and sixteen corresponding guides to the existing eight games and nine stories. The published catalog has twenty-four games and twenty-five stories.

## New coverage

- Elden Ring: planning a manageable first expedition.
- Baldur's Gate 3: evaluating actions, positions and enemy turns.
- Cyberpunk 2077: choosing a play style across the different platform versions.
- Black Myth: Wukong: learning from successive boss attempts.
- Monster Hunter Wilds: testing weapon reach, commitment and resource demands.
- Hades II: choosing boons for Attack, Special, Cast and charged Omega actions.
- Hollow Knight: Silksong: keeping useful exploration and route notes.
- Stardew Valley: balancing the first spring's farm work with the town.
- Helldivers 2: squad communication, friendly fire and extraction.
- Palworld: a base with workable assignments, paths, food and rest.
- Sea of Thieves: sailing, ship roles, repairs and a first voyage.
- No Man's Sky: survival systems, repairs and a safe first journey.
- Forza Horizon 5: assists, braking, corner exits and repeatable comparisons.
- Civilization VII: planning across Ages under the current Test of Time rules.
- Street Fighter 6: inputs, defense, anti-air practice and a focused match goal.
- EA SPORTS FC 26: a coherent first Manager Career squad and match approach.

Game metadata and overviews live in the two expansion editorial modules. Each guide has primary source links, a stable ID and URL, a relationship to its game, and a reading-time estimate calculated from its actual body. New editorial records use score zero; the public review score continues to come from player ratings.

The associated official screenshot catalog is documented in [catalog-expansion-media.md](catalog-expansion-media.md). Full article figures preserve the image proportions; game cards and article thumbnails apply their own crops. Article media uses the linked game ID as a fallback, so publication IDs do not have to encode a game name.

## Publication checks

The catalog integrity checks reject duplicate IDs/URLs, orphaned story links, missing game coverage and missing image files. Every game has an overview and every story has a cover. Runtime checks also inspect image dimensions, public detail pages, linked coverage, search, genre filters, image loading and sitemap entries.

Sitemap modification dates now use the content's update timestamp rather than a game's historical or announced launch date. Image URLs are included for the game and story pages.

The working checkout contained unrelated ad-viewer edits when this work began. Catalog work was performed in an isolated managed worktree; those edits were not included in the release.
