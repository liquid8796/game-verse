# RPG story body illustrations

Selected and checked on October 8, 2026. `inline-rpg.ts` supplies thirteen contextual body figures across the eight RPG starter stories: eleven official gallery screenshots and two original explanatory SVG diagrams. Every screenshot is different from that story's existing header cover, including the original CDN asset.

The whole story was read before choosing its figures. Placements use the exact section heading plus a one-based paragraph number within that section. The figure belongs immediately after the discussion it illustrates, with the surrounding paragraphs continuing afterward where appropriate.

## Screenshot sources and placement

All eleven originals came from the `path_full` field in each game's public Steam store gallery API. The Steam pages below are the developer or publisher's official listings. Full-size originals were downloaded, proportionally resized to a maximum width of 1600 pixels and converted with Sharp WebP quality 85. There was no crop, generative alteration, UI removal or content replacement. Each saved screenshot is 1600 × 900. Captions and alt text were written after visual inspection of the selected files.

The gallery does not identify a capture build or date. In particular, Baldur's Gate 3 shows an older party/interface presentation, and Hades II's gallery spans a game that previously ran in Early Access. These files illustrate visible situations; they do not establish current patch behavior, a recommended build, the exact resource cost of an ability, or the timing of a boss attack. The Cyberpunk 2077 still is likewise not labeled as post-2.0 proof. Captions avoid those claims.
